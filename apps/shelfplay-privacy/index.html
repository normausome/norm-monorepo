ShelfPlay Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

ShelfPlay is a local-files-only audiobook player for iOS. This page describes how the app handles information.

Data we collect

ShelfPlay does not require an account. We do not collect personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash reports through third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Your audio files

Audiobooks and other audio you import stay on your device. ShelfPlay does not upload your files to our servers. We do not operate cloud storage or sync for your library.

Tracking

ShelfPlay does not use tracking, analytics, or advertising. This website does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts.

Third parties

ShelfPlay does not share your data with third parties for marketing or analytics. Apple’s App Store may process purchase or download information according to Apple’s privacy policy; that is outside ShelfPlay’s control.

Children

ShelfPlay is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
