# Load calculation

These figures are estimates from the job notes, late September 2026.

The notes use an optional-method style. The notes say Norman or Sparky check the numbers again before anyone relies on them. This page records those working estimates.

## Line items in the notes

- Dishwasher, about 1,500 volt-amperes.
- Disposal, about 900 volt-amperes.
- Air conditioner, Payne model PA13NA030. The notes name the model. They do not give a separate volt-ampere figure for it.
- Tankless water heater, assumed 27 kilowatts.
- Universal Wall Connector, 11,520 volt-amperes.

The microwave is on a small-appliance circuit. The notes exclude the microwave from this framing.

## Total in the notes

The notes total about 45,152 volt-amperes. The notes call that about 188 A on the 200 A service.

The line items omit a volt-ampere figure for the Payne air conditioner. The 45,152 volt-ampere total is the figure written in the notes. This page does not recompute that total from the line items.
